Delete your PisoPulse account

You can delete your PisoPulse account at any time. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  1. Open PisoPulse and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.
  5. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

Option 2: Can't open the app? Ask us to delete it

If you uninstalled the app or forgot your password, email us from the email address registered to your account:

pisopulse.app@gmail.com

  • Use the subject Delete my PisoPulse account.
  • Include your PisoPulse username.
  • Send it from the same email address you signed up with, so we can confirm it is you.

We will delete your account and reply within 14 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username and display name.
  • Every expense you logged.
  • Your privacy settings.
  • Your friendships and friend requests. Friends will no longer see you.
  • Your login details (your email address and password).

Copies may remain in our backups for a limited period before they expire. After deletion, your username and email can be used to sign up again.
